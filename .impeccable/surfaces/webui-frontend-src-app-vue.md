## Scope
Flandora browser console, all seven existing pages. Operate mode for operators working in ordinary indoor desktop light and sometimes on phones.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A quiet WeChat-colored operations canvas, not a stack of pastel cards. A connected service strip and grouped diagnostic workspace make the bridge legible immediately.

OWN-WORLD: WeChat green, cool white work surfaces, pale gray-green navigation; near-black surfaces in dark mode. Compact system typography, fine neutral rules, restrained elevated cards, solid green selected states, one consistent line-icon system. SnowLuma's collapsible rail, inset canvas and compact controls translated into WeChat's palette.

STORY: The operator first sees the page name and connection, then whether the bridge is serving traffic and which control to use. Messages, live desktop and logs support diagnosis; grouped settings keep advanced configuration accessible without obscuring routine work.

FIRST VIEWPORT: 240px grouped navigation beside an inset top bar and a fluid content canvas. Dashboard title and a compact service/action panel precede four state tiles; activity and contacts share the next row. Primary action is contextual to actual service state. Mobile uses a modal navigation drawer, single-column content and accessible 44px controls.

FORM: User-pinned SnowLuma management-console structure and WeChat color grammar override the candidate order. Seed 92f1ec83. The six challengers lose product clarity; retain the timetable's tabular discipline, folio's meaningful step sequence, ticket wallet's visible journey state, factory catalog's consistent state codes and exposure record's aligned data cells. Character shelf conflicts with operational content and contributes no rule.

SIGNATURE: Navigation selection moves with a short eased transition; the rail collapses without shifting its icons; theme and drawer controls have explicit state. Reduced motion removes these transitions.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
